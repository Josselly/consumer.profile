Orbit needs your approval to read visit dates, page titles, and page URLs from Chrome history. The dashboard lets you choose 7, 30, or 90 days.

Records go only to josselly.github.io/consumer.profile/ in this browser. They are analyzed in memory and never uploaded. No passwords, cookies, or account tokens are accessed.

Chrome’s history permission technically permits history changes; Orbit only reads history and never changes or deletes it.
